This is the Oracle, the name is based on the Percy Jackson and the Olympian's Oracle at the attic of the Camp Half-Blood. It will be the stream bot for my live chats.

## The Commands

- `!oracle github` - Shares https://swarn.space/github in the chat 
- `!oracle  twitter` - Shares https://swarn.space/twitter in the chat
- `Automoderation` - Delets offensive words based on our offensive.db sqlite3 database, or timesout someone who's been spamming the same message (starting for spam: 10 messages)

More commands will come soon, till then these will be the basic ones we need as I will mostly be doing development streams and recreational programming.
